What the card shows

  • No numbers. The target stays hidden (pillar 1).
  • Decoded keywords are underlined and carry their trait badge.
  • Intensity words get a dotted line once you've learned them.
  • Portraits share one construction: ink outline, flat fills, one accent colour.

Plate VI

A working mock-up of the main screen at phone width. Tap shelf items to fill the slots, tap a slot to empty it, switch Simmer or Boil, then brew for Marla.

  • The meter is an honest preview: hatched bars and “?” mark what the Notebook doesn't know yet. Try the glowcap.
  • Boiling a delicate flower costs Calm, and simmering a tough root costs its main trait. Wren notes each rule the first time it happens.
  • All tap targets are at least 44 px and text is 16 px or larger.
